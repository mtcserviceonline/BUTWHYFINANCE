# Prompt per Google Flow (Veo), con la voce dell'orso

Ogni prompt è completo: stile, azione, battuta (in italiano, numeri scritti in lettere per la pronuncia) e audio.
Una clip di Veo dura al massimo 8 secondi, quindi le battute lunghe sono divise in due parti: la parte A parte dal primo fotogramma, la parte B si fa con **Estendi** sulla parte A.

Voce, uguale in ogni prompt: `a warm, friendly young adult male voice speaking natural Italian with a native Italian accent, clear and calm, medium pace`.

## SCENA 1 · Il paradosso
Primo fotogramma: `fotogrammi/01.png`
```
Modern comic-book cartoon animation, bold clean black outlines, flat cel-shaded colors, sunny Italian residential street with two identical small houses, warm daylight, vertical 9:16. The bear from the reference image (brown cartoon bear, black round sunglasses always on, dark green t-shirt with a yellow euro sign) puts the house key in his pocket, picks up two blank price tags, one large and one small, and lifts the small one higher than the large one, looking at the camera puzzled with one eyebrow raised. Slight push-in on his face. While doing this he talks to the camera, his mouth moving in sync with the words, curious tone, in Italian: "Affittare una casa a ottocento euro può rendere più che affittarla a mille... Ma perché?" Voice: a warm, friendly young adult male voice speaking natural Italian with a native Italian accent, clear and calm, medium pace. Audio: only his voice and soft street ambience, no music. No subtitles, no on-screen text, no other characters.
```
