# Prompt delle scene: stile fumetto

Riferimenti da allegare sempre: `brand/mascotte/mascotte-principale.jpg` (orso) e, solo per lo stile, `script/stile/stile-1.png` e `stile-2.png`.
Primo fotogramma di ogni scena: `fotogrammi/01.png` … `06.png`.

## STILE

```
Style: modern comic-book cartoon illustration, bold clean black ink outlines, flat cel-shaded colors with soft simple shading, detailed semi-realistic Italian background drawn in the same comic style, warm natural daylight, slightly saturated warm palette, vertical 9:16. The bear: brown cartoon bear with copper-orange fur and light peach muzzle, black round sunglasses with a white glare always on, dark green t-shirt with a big yellow euro sign, same design and proportions as the reference image, fully visible. He is the only character. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no flags; all papers, tags, receipts and documents are blank.
```

## SCENA 1 · Il paradosso
Primo fotogramma: `fotogrammi/01.png`. Una via italiana soleggiata, l'orso con la chiave davanti a due case identiche, curioso.
Ultimo fotogramma:
```
Sunny Italian residential street with two identical small houses side by side. The bear holds up two blank price tags, a small one lifted higher than a large one, with a puzzled smile and one eyebrow raised above the sunglasses.
```
Prompt scena:
```
The bear puts the house key in his pocket, picks up two blank price tags, one large and one small, lifts the small one higher than the large one and looks at the camera puzzled, one eyebrow raised. Slight push-in on his face. Smooth cartoon animation, the two identical houses stay in the background.
```
Voce ElevenLabs: `[curious] Affittare una casa a 800 euro può rendere più che affittarla a 1.000. [short pause] Ma perché?`

Ultimo fotogramma:
```
Bright office. The bear stands beside a large blank document taped to the wall with masking tape, pointing at it with one paw; three rubber ink stamps of decreasing size stand on a small wooden table below, one blank sheet with a small round ink mark next to them.
```
Prompt scena:
```
The bear sets the price tags down, walks to the large blank document taped to the wall and points at it, then glances down at the three rubber ink stamps of decreasing size on the small table, picks up the smallest one and stamps a small round ink mark on a blank sheet. Slow sideways tracking shot. Smooth cartoon animation.
```
Voce ElevenLabs: `[calm] La manovra propone: tasse all'1% su chi affitta a canone concordato a chi ha fino a 35 anni. [short pause] Oggi sono al 10%. [short pause] Sul mercato libero, al 21%.`
